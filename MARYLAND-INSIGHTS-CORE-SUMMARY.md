## Maryland Insights — Core Summary

**Maryland Insights is a Maryland-rooted, open GTM platform for Maryland businesses and marketing agencies.**

Its visible entry product is **Website + Hosting**—but not a commodity website or generic builder. It is a **website designed to convert, create measurable business value, and grow the business**.

The real product is the platform behind every website:

- Maryland-specific GTM strategy and local-market intelligence
- Discovery, local visibility, lead capture, conversion, automation, analytics, and growth systems
- Tailored tools, integrations, and custom workflows for each business or agency
- Agency-grade infrastructure that lets Maryland marketing agencies deliver materially stronger client GTM experiences

## Customers

| Segment | What Maryland Insights delivers |
|---|---|
| Maryland businesses | A conversion-focused website and hosting foundation, powered by a tailored GTM system that drives ongoing growth |
| Maryland marketing agencies | An open, configurable platform for delivering managed websites, localized GTM workflows, reporting, conversion infrastructure, and better client outcomes |

## Brand position

Maryland Insights must feel **professional, durable, deeply local, and institutionally credible**—near state-government-level solidity, not a flashy new AI product or a generic hosting provider.

It communicates:

- Maryland focus, partnerships, and local accountability
- Serious technology infrastructure and long-term reliability
- Modern GTM systems without superficial startup hype
- A trusted business-growth partner, not just a website vendor

## Open platform

AI supports the platform but is **not** its headline product and is not locked behind artificial usage credits.

Maryland Insights is model- and tool-agnostic: customers and agencies can access and integrate the systems appropriate to their workflows, including Claude, Codex, OpenClaw, custom harnesses, and A2A-connected agents.

**Core positioning:**
> Maryland Insights provides conversion-focused websites and hosting, powered by an open, Maryland-specific GTM platform that helps businesses and agencies build durable, measurable growth.
