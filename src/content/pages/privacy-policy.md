#### Who We Are

Maryland Insights ("we," "us," "our") provides website platforms, hosting, local SEO, and growth tools for Maryland small businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through marylandinsights.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website only.

#### Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to share with us:

- **Contact and lead forms:** your name, email address, phone number (optional), and the contents of your message, including business details you provide such as your business name or Google Place ID.
- **Newsletter signup:** your email address.

We do not require you to create an account, and this site has no login or password system.

#### How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

- Respond to your inquiries and follow up on quote or demo requests.
- Send newsletters and updates you signed up for. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
- Operate, maintain, and improve our website and services.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

#### How Your Information Is Stored and Shared

Form submissions on this site are sent to our own customer relationship system at crm.marylandinsights.com so our team can respond to you. We do not share your information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

#### Cookies and Tracking

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party analytics on this site. The site may use basic browser storage strictly necessary for forms and site functionality to work.

#### Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes described above — for example, to respond to an inquiry or while you remain subscribed to our newsletter — and then delete it or anonymize it.

#### Your Rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

#### Children's Privacy

This site is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

#### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
