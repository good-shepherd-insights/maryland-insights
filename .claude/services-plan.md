## What top local-marketing sites do (research)
- **Scorpion** groups offerings into **3 pillars** (Technology / Advertising / Marketing), then organizes the rest **by industry** (Home Services → HVAC, plumbing, roofing…; Legal → practice areas), each linking to its own page. Source: scorpion.co
- Top agency sites use **the customer's own vocabulary** ("plumbing leads", not "digital marketing services") and send each offering to a **deep dedicated page**. Source: nanoglobals.com/marketing-agency-websites
- **Measured results beat claims**: revenue/lead numbers + named case studies separate top converters. Sources: Scorpion, nanoglobals
- **No carousels**, services in visitor-priority order, links to service pages not just contact. Source: orbitmedia.com
- **One-line service blurbs = thin content**; each service needs a real descriptive paragraph. Source: monkeyvision.co
