# About Us, polished-marketing version (negative example)

This is the "polished marketing" version of the About section. It is the negative example that demonstrates what the founder's voice is not. It was drafted first and rejected in session because it reads like any other GTM platform's About page.

Keep this on hand so future drafts do not drift back into this register.

## The rejected draft

Maryland Insights is a GTM infrastructure platform built by data science and go-to-market engineers. Founded in 2015, we serve Maryland businesses and agencies by providing the foundational tools, hosting, maintenance, and integrations needed to scale. Unlike traditional marketing agencies or SaaS tools, we focus on augmentation over replacement, empowering our partners to make intelligent, data-driven decisions at the state level.

## What fails

- **Generic cadence.** Could appear on any platform's About page.
- **No signature phrases.** "Augmentation over replacement" is the only load-bearing phrase, and it is embedded in a generic sentence rather than landing as a beat.
- **No three-beat negative.** The piece defines Maryland Insights by what it provides, not by what it is not.
- **"Empowering our partners."** Model-default verb. Not the founder's voice.
- **"Intelligent, data-driven decisions."** Model-default adjective stack.
- **No verbatim founder phrasing.** The closest phrase ("augmentation over replacement") is rendered as a subordinate clause instead of a beat.
- **Founder voice is gone.** The reader could not tell this from any other platform's About page.

## Why it conflicts with this writing

The voice rules in `VOICE.md` ban polished-marketing cadence, polished-marketing verbs, and any sentence that could appear on any GTM platform's About page. This draft fails all three tests.

The structural rules in `STYLE.md` require the boundary-drawing move ("we are not X, not Y, not Z") in the first 200 words of an About or Services page. This draft never draws the line.

## Preferred direction

Rewrite in stacked declaratives. Use the founder's three-beat negative identity pattern. Preserve the verbatim "augmentation, not replacement" phrase and let it close a paragraph. Drop "empowering" and similar model defaults. See `about-us-right-version.md` for the working draft.

## What to learn from this example

When a draft sounds like it could appear on another company's site, it is wrong. When it sounds like the founder said it on a good day, it is right. That is the calibration bar.
