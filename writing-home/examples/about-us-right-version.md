# About Us, right version

This is the working "right version" of the About section, drafted from the source transcript. It demonstrates the three-beat negative identity, the foundation-layer framing, the verbatim augmentation phrase, and the stacked-declarative rhythm.

Use this as the reference for tone and structure when drafting About, Services, or homepage copy.

## The draft

We're a team of data science and go-to-market engineers. We've been working on this problem since 2015, what it actually takes to grow and scale a local business.

We're not a marketing agency. We're not a SaaS platform. We're not a bespoke software agency. We're the foundation layer.

We build the infrastructure that Maryland agencies and the businesses they serve can plug into. Not to replace what they have. To make what they have finally work the way it was supposed to. Augmentation, not replacement.

Our main focus is Maryland agencies. They are already successful. They are already working with Maryland businesses. They are already data-oriented. We give them the engineering-grade tools that the enterprises have, applied at the state level, so they can move faster and scale more without spending their time juggling tooling that was not built for them.

## Where each piece came from

- "data science and go-to-market engineers": verbatim from transcript.
- "since 2015, what it actually takes to grow and scale a local business": verbatim.
- "We're not a marketing agency. We're not a SaaS platform. We're not a bespoke software agency.": verbatim triple negative, kept in original order.
- "the foundation layer": the founder's own phrase for the platform's role.
- "Not to replace what they have. To make what they have finally work the way it was supposed to.": close paraphrase of the augmentation argument, restructured into the founder's declarative rhythm.
- "Augmentation, not replacement": verbatim signature phrase, used as the closing kicker.
- "Our main focus is Maryland agencies. They are already successful. They are already working with Maryland businesses. They are already data-oriented.": the three-beat pattern applied to the audience framing, drawn directly from the transcript's argument.

## What this example demonstrates

- The three-beat negative identity move (see `VOICE.md`).
- The augmentation restatement (see `VOICE.md`).
- The Maryland boundary, drawn in the first paragraph.
- Engineering-grade framing applied at the state level, anchored in the founder's actual words.
- No em-dashes. No marketing-website cadence. No "we believe" hedging.

## What not to imitate mechanically

- The paragraph breaks. The rhythm matters. The exact line breaks are not sacred and will vary by surface.
- The exact verbs. "Plug into" works here because the platform relationship is the point. Other verbs (use, leverage, run on top of) would change the meaning.
